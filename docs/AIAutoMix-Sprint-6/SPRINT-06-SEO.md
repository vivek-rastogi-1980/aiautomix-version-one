# Sprint 06 — SEO Specification

## Brand
AIAutoMix  
Canonical: `https://www.aiautomix.com`

Positioning:
> AI-powered business intelligence, automation, AI agents and business growth solutions.

## Homepage
### Title
`AI Automation Agency & AI Business Solutions | AIAutoMix`

### Description
`Transform your business with AI automation, AI agents, CRM, voice AI, business intelligence, and custom AI solutions. AIAutoMix helps businesses automate, grow, and scale.`

## Metadata
Every public page should have:
- unique title
- unique description
- canonical
- OG title/description/url/image
- Twitter/X metadata

## Robots
Allow public pages. Disallow private application routes and endpoints where appropriate.

## Sitemap
Include only canonical, public, indexable pages. Exclude dashboards, admin, login, signup, API and duplicate URLs.

## Structured Data
Use only accurate visible-content schema:
- Organization
- WebSite
- WebPage
- Service
- BreadcrumbList
- Article

Avoid fake reviews, ratings and unsupported claims.

## Recommended Content Architecture

Do not create thin pages only for keywords.

## Internal Linking
Link naturally between related:
- services
- industries
- tools
- insights
- consultation CTAs

## International SEO
Target USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Europe and India without creating fake local presence or thin country pages.

## Search Console
After production:
1. Verify domain.
2. Submit sitemap.
3. Inspect homepage.
4. Inspect key service pages.
5. Monitor indexing.
6. Monitor Core Web Vitals.
7. Monitor queries and impressions.
