> An AI-powered business intelligence and automation platform helping businesses validate, build, automate and scale.

## Editorial Rule
AI-generated content must not automatically publish.

Recommended:
```text
Trend Research
  ↓
Research
  ↓
Fact Check
  ↓
Draft
  ↓
SEO Review
  ↓
Image
  ↓
Boss/Editor Agent
  ↓
Human Approval
  ↓
Publish
```

Human approval remains mandatory.

## SEO Rule
Do not create content merely because a keyword exists. Every article should answer:
1. What is the user's intent?
2. What useful original insight can AIAutoMix provide?
3. What action should the reader take next?
4. Which AIAutoMix tool/service is naturally relevant?
