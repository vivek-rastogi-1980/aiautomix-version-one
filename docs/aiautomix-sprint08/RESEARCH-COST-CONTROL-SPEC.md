# MARKET RESEARCH COST CONTROL SPECIFICATION

## Objective
Prevent uncontrolled AI and research costs.

## Controls
- Research depth tiers
- Maximum sources per stage and run
- Token/input limits
- Stage timeouts
- Retry limits
- Duplicate-source elimination
- Cache/reuse where appropriate
- Credit preflight
- Cost estimation
- Admin visibility

## Suggested Depths
Basic — small source set and concise report.
Standard — broader research and stronger evidence coverage.
Deep — largest permitted research budget and source set.

Exact limits must be centrally configured.
