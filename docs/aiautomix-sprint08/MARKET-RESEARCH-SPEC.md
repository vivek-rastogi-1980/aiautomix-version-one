# MARKET RESEARCH ENGINE SPECIFICATION

## Purpose
Provide evidence-backed market intelligence for a user's business idea.

## Inputs
Business idea, business plan, industry, geography, target customer, business model, research questions, optional competitors and depth.

## Outputs
Structured Market Research Report plus sources, evidence, confidence and limitations.

## Required Sections
Executive Summary; Research Scope & Methodology; Market Overview; Market Size & Growth Evidence; Target Customer / ICP; Customer Problems & Needs; Industry Trends; Demand Signals; Business Model & Pricing Signals; Opportunities; Risks & Challenges; Regulatory/Environmental Considerations; Strategic Recommendations; Evidence & Sources; Confidence / Limitations.

## Principle
A claim without sufficient evidence must be qualified rather than presented as fact.
